Govt Schemes
Explore central and Andhra Pradesh state government schemes, subsidies, and incentives for solar and wind energy adoption.
Central Govt Schemes
Central
PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana
Rooftop solar subsidy scheme offering up to 300 units of free electricity per month for households that install rooftop solar systems.
Subsidy: Up to ₹78,000 depending on system size Eligible: Residential rooftop solar (1-3 kW+)
Central
PM-KUSUM Yojana
Supports farmers with solar pumps and solar power plants on barren land, reducing diesel dependency for irrigation.
Subsidy: 60% central+state subsidy, 30% loan, 10% farmer share Eligible: Farmers, cooperatives, panchayats
Andhra Pradesh State Schemes
AP State
AP Solar Rooftop Subsidy Program
State-level top-up incentives for residential and MSME rooftop solar installations, aligned with the central PM Surya Ghar scheme.
Subsidy: Varies by discom (APSPDCL / APEPDCL) Eligible: AP residents and small businesses
AP State
AP Wind & Solar Hybrid Policy
Encourages hybrid wind-solar power projects in AP with land allotment support and simplified clearances for developers.
Benefit: Land/grid connectivity support, tax incentives Eligible: Commercial/industrial renewable developers
Scheme details, subsidy amounts, and eligibility change frequently. Always confirm current terms on the official PM Surya Ghar portal or your local AP DISCOM office before applying.
